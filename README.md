## 📖 About HanTang Codex

**HanTang Codex (汉唐方典)** is a next-generation intelligent clinical decision and retrieval system designed for Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) practitioners, acupuncture professionals, classical formula (Jingfang) scholars, and integrative health researchers worldwide.

The platform systematically integrates the classical canons of Chinese medicine—specifically the *Shanghan Lun* (Treatise on Cold Damage Diseases) and *Jingui Yaolue* (Essential Prescriptions from the Golden Cabinet) by the Sage of Medicine **Zhang Zhongjing**—alongside the clinical pearls and *Renji* (人纪) lectures of contemporary master **Ni Haixia (倪海厦)**.

## ✨ Core Features

### 1. 🔍 Intelligent Bilingual Search & Classical Sourcing
- **391 Canonical Formulas**: Full coverage across the Six Stages (Taiyang, Yangming, Shaoyang, Taiyin, Shaoyin, Jueyin) and Master Ni's proven clinical formulas (HT-1 through HT-100).
- **Plain-Language Indications**: Every formula includes plain-language indications and modern clinical correlations alongside ancient source texts.
- **Direct Classical Text Sourcing**: Accurate cross-referencing with classical canons.

### 2. 🎯 Multi-Symptom Weighted Pattern Radar (50+ Symptoms / 8 Bodily Systems)
- **Multi-Select Composite Input**: Select 3 to 5 simultaneous bodily complaints (e.g., *Cold extremities + Abdominal discomfort + Insomnia*).
- **Real-Time Weighted Match Score**: Automatically evaluates the database and calculates match scores (`🔥 100% Match` / `🎯 75% Match`), sorting the most aligned canonical formulas by clinical relevance.

### 3. ⚖️ Formula Comparator & Pathology Evolution Matrix (Key Feature)
- **Side-by-Side Differentiation**: Select 2 to 4 easily confused related formulas (e.g., *Guizhi Decoction vs. Mahuang Decoction vs. Gegen Decoction*).
- **Herb Modification Differential Table**: Clear visualization of shared base herbs (`[Base Herbs]`) versus modified herbs (`[Modifications]`) with metric dosage comparisons.
- **🎯 Key Deciding Factor**: Direct clinical criteria to decisively distinguish between similar formulas.
- **👤 Typical Patient Persona**: Vivid real-world scenarios to immediately identify the target pattern.
- **🔘 Interactive Differential Guide**: One-click quick triage to highlight the exact indicated formula.

### 4. 📜 Standardized Botanical Prescription Generation & Export
- **Standardized Prescription Layout**: Formatted with formula source, prescription date, and clear dosage tables.
- **3-Column Botanical Nomenclature**: Standard Chinese herb name, Pinyin with tone marks, **Pharmaceutical Latin Nomenclature** (*e.g., Ramulus Cinnamomi, Radix Glycyrrhizae*), and precise metric grams (`g`).
- **3-Step Decoction & Administration Protocol**: Clear graphical instructions for preparation (`PREPARE`), boiling (`BOIL`), and dosage timing (`ADMINISTER`).
- **Multi-Format Export**: One-click export to high-resolution prescription images (ideal for mobile sharing), clean A4 printable layouts, and raw text clipboard copy.

### 5. ⚠️ Herb Safety & Contraindication Engine
- **Incompatibility Screening**: Automatic detection of classical *Eighteen Incompatibilities (十八反)* and *Nineteen Counteractions (十九畏)*.
- **Special Population Warnings**: Automatic safety alerts for pregnancy and fragile constitutions when formulas contain potent herbs (e.g., *Aconitum/附子, Rheum/大黄, Natrii Sulfas/芒硝*).
- **Decoction Technique Warnings**: Essential guidance on special preparations (e.g., pre-boil Mahuang, decoct Radix Aconiti Lateralis Praeparata for extended duration, dissolve Natrii Sulfas separately).
